Photo tools

Tools for the shop's photographs, in the house style. They run entirely in your browser: photos are never uploaded.

Tuned by part

Prints a batch of photos in fine or story ink with the toned colour, one set of settings for them all, and tunes the colour part by part: the product, generic gear, skin and the background. Each photo splits itself into parts, or takes them from a parts map you painted in colours of your own, and you correct them with a brush, a lasso or a click. The background can be printed, removed or filled, and every photo is saved at its own size.

D116 inks, D121 toned colour, Q60 tuning.
