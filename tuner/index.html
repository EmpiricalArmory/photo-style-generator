    About

    The inks are D116 and the toned colour D121, exactly as in the decisions doc; each part gets its own strength (Q60). Steel prints as ink.

    The split runs on your machine: ormbg (Apache-2.0) finds the subject, YOLOS tiny (Apache-2.0) finds people and SlimSAM (Apache-2.0) selects what you click, through Transformers.js and ONNX Runtime Web.

    Shortcuts: B brush, L lasso, C click, H hand, 1–5 parts, [ and ] brush size, X erase, R / P / O views, \ hold to compare, F fit, Space to move, Cmd/Ctrl+Z undo.
